Privacy Policy for Draw Color Art Pro

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

1. Introduction and Overview
Draw Color Art Pro is developed by SHARP OP as an advanced mobile drawing, sketching, painting, and digital coloring application. This Privacy Policy outlines our uncompromising commitment to your privacy and details how our application handles your information.

Our guiding principle is simple: your creative work belongs solely to you, and your personal privacy is paramount. Draw Color Art Pro is built with a strict local-first, privacy-by-design philosophy. We do not track you, we do not profile your usage, and we do not collect, sell, or rent your personal information.

2. Information Collection and Processing
Draw Color Art Pro does not collect, harvest, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.

- No Account Required: You can access and enjoy all features of the app without creating an account, registering an email address, or logging in.
- No Personal Identification: We never ask for your name, phone number, address, contact lists, or social media profiles.
- No Telemetry or Behavioral Tracking: We do not deploy third-party analytics trackers, background telemetry agents, or behavioral advertising identifiers.
- No Cloud Synchronization by Default: All your artwork, drawings, color swatches, custom brushes, and layer compositions are processed and kept strictly on your local device.

3. Device Permissions and Usage
To deliver professional digital art features, Draw Color Art Pro may request certain device permissions. Each permission is utilized solely for immediate, on-device creative functionality:

- Photos and Media Storage: Used exclusively when you choose to import a background reference image onto your canvas or when you choose to save and export your finished artwork in PNG or JPEG format to your device gallery.
- Camera: Used exclusively for the optional AR Tracing feature, allowing you to project and trace reference outlines onto physical paper through your device screen. Camera feed processing happens entirely in real time in temporary device memory. No video feeds or photos from your camera are ever recorded, saved, or uploaded to any server.
- Audio and Haptic Feedback: Used to play interactive sound effects (such as pencil sketching, airbrush spraying, and paper eraser sounds) and provide tactile click feedback.
- Network and Internet: Used solely if you choose to browse or download open Google Fonts for typography styling, or if you explicitly use the system share sheet to share your artwork through other apps installed on your device.

4. Artwork and Project Data Storage
All projects, layers, strokes, undo and redo history states, and custom color swatches are stored in your device local application storage directory. We have no access to your projects. If you choose to share or export your artwork, it is handled directly through your operating system standard share sheet or saved to your local gallery upon your explicit command.

5. Children Privacy and Family Safety
Draw Color Art Pro is suitable for all ages, including children and families. In strict compliance with the Children Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and international child safety standards:

- We do not collect personal information from children or any users.
- We do not display personalized or targeted behavioral advertisements.
- We do not include social chat rooms, direct messaging, or public forums.
- The app provides a safe, private, and distraction-free creative environment for young artists and students.

6. Data Retention and Deletion
Because all your data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over its retention:

- You can delete individual drawings or clear project caches at any time within the app settings.
- If you uninstall Draw Color Art Pro from your device, all locally stored project files and application data are immediately and permanently removed by your operating system.

7. Third-Party Services
Draw Color Art Pro does not share your data with third-party marketing or advertising networks. The app uses standard platform libraries provided by Apple and Google for font rendering and standard operating system file dialogs. These platform components operate under their respective privacy policies.

8. Security
We care deeply about the security of your creative work. By keeping all processing and storage entirely local on your device, we eliminate the risks associated with remote database breaches, unauthorized server access, and data leaks.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new features or legal compliance requirements. Any revisions will be published with an updated Last Updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
